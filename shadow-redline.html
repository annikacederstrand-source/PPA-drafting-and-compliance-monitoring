§ 12.6 EGS Reservoir Performance — Failure & Remedy
⚠ CRITICAL EGS-SPECIFIC TECH EXPOSURE

A "Reservoir Performance Failure" shall be deemed to occur if the Project fails to achieve ninety percent (90%) of the Guaranteed Capacity for a period of thirty (30) consecutive days ninety percent (90%) of the Guaranteed Capacity for a period of ninety (90) consecutive days, excluding any period during which a documented well workover, stimulation remediation, or Transmission Provider-caused outage is in progress. Upon declaration of a Reservoir Performance Failure, Buyer may terminate this Agreement immediately and without liability to Seller provide written notice to Seller initiating a 180-day Cure Period during which Seller may undertake reservoir remediation measures; Buyer's termination right shall not arise until expiration of the Cure Period without successful remediation. ⚠ FLAG #1

// TECH-TO-LEGAL EXPOSURE — FLAG #1 — EGS-SPECIFIC
This clause has no equivalent in Fervo's prior geothermal or wind/solar PPA corpus — it is novel to EGS and represents the highest-priority drafting risk on this agreement. A 30-day consecutive underperformance window is operationally unrealistic for EGS: reservoir pressure fluctuations, scale deposition, and workover operations routinely create performance dips of 30–60 days that fully resolve without permanent capacity loss. Immediate termination right with no cure period would allow Buyer to exit a fully functional project during a routine maintenance window. Recommend: 90-day window minimum + 180-day cure period + explicit workover carve-out. Flag for engineering review before redline goes out.
§ 12.4 Force Majeure — EGS Stimulation & Interconnection Events
⚠ CRITICAL TECH EXPOSURE PRIOR: PROJ. RED

Force Majeure shall include, without limitation, acts of God, labor disputes, and delays caused by transmission service interruptions not exceeding fifteen (15) days, and subsurface conditions materially different from those represented in Seller's geologic studies not exceeding thirty (30) days delays caused by transmission service interruptions; subsurface conditions materially different from pre-stimulation geologic surveys, including unexpected formation permeability, induced seismicity requiring operational pause per applicable regulatory protocol, or fluid loss events — each without cap on duration — provided that Seller provides written notice within ten (10) days of the triggering event and monthly status reports thereafter. ⚠ FLAG #2

// TECH-TO-LEGAL EXPOSURE — FLAG #2 — EGS STIMULATION RISK
Hard 15/30-day caps on EGS-specific force majeure events are operationally indefensible. Subsurface conditions in EGS greenfield development (Project Breckenridge is a first-of-kind Colorado formation) can deviate materially from pre-drill surveys with no predictable resolution timeline. Induced seismicity pause requirements under Colorado COGCC Rule 503(c) alone can mandate operational stops of 60–120 days pending regulatory review — entirely outside Seller's control. Precedent from Project Red Station 1 negotiation with NV Energy: open-ended FM extension secured for all subsurface events. Recommend importing Project Red § 12.4 language verbatim as the baseline position.
§ 5.1 Interconnection — Queue Position & Rights
⚠ FERC RM22-14 ORDER 2023-A

Seller warrants that it holds a valid interconnection queue position as of the Effective Date and shall maintain such position throughout the negotiation period. In the event of queue position forfeiture for any reason, Buyer may terminate this Agreement without liability. use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain such position. Queue position changes resulting from FERC Order 2023 cluster study reforms, reconfigurations, or Transmission Provider-directed modifications shall not constitute grounds for termination, and Buyer's termination right in this section shall not apply during any period of active interconnection cluster study or pending FERC dispute resolution.

// FERC ORDER 2023-A — ISSUED APR 22, 2026 · APPLIES TO BRECKENRIDGE QUEUE POSITION
Supplemental guidance under FERC RM22-14 clarifies that cluster study reconfigurations do not constitute voluntary queue withdrawal. Project Breckenridge's SPP queue position (Colorado interconnection) is currently in active cluster study — the unamended draft language would give Buyer a termination right based on a Transmission Provider action explicitly now outside Seller's control under the new Order. Attorney review required before redlines go out. Note: SPP cluster study timelines in Colorado have run 18–24 months under the new reform process — this exposure is live and material.
§ 8.2 Scheduling, Dispatch & Curtailment — EGS Baseload
HIST. TENSION EGS-SPECIFIC SCHED. IMPL.

Seller shall schedule energy delivery in accordance with Buyer's dispatch instructions, including curtailment requests. Buyer may curtail deliveries without limitation and without obligation to compensate Seller for curtailed energy volumes. Buyer may curtail deliveries provided that: (i) curtailment compensation shall be paid at the Contract Price for any curtailment exceeding five percent (5%) of monthly scheduled energy, reflecting EGS baseload operational constraints; (ii) economic curtailment requested solely for Buyer's portfolio optimization shall be expressly distinguished from reliability curtailment in any dispatch instruction; (iii) Seller shall receive no less than four (4) hours advance notice of non-emergency curtailment; and (iv) no curtailment instruction shall require Seller to operate below the minimum reservoir circulation flow rate specified in Exhibit D (EGS Operating Parameters).

// HISTORICAL TENSION POINT + EGS ADDITION — PAINTED ROCK GEO / PROJECT RED
Uncapped curtailment without compensation is the standard Buyer opening position and a recurring tension point. Painted Rock Geo (Rocky Mountain Power, 2024): secured 10% monthly compensation trigger after 2 rounds. Project Red Station 1 (NV Energy, 2025): secured 5% threshold given EGS baseload economics — recommend using 5% as the baseline for Breckenridge. New EGS-specific addition (iv) is critical: EGS systems require continuous fluid circulation to maintain reservoir temperature and pressure — a curtailment instruction that drives output below minimum circulation flow can cause irreversible reservoir damage not recoverable under § 12.6. This language has no precedent in the Fervo corpus and should be reviewed by the engineering team before inclusion.
§ 3.5 Commercial Operation Date — EGS Stimulation Milestones & Extensions
SCHEDULING IMPL. EGS-SPECIFIC

The Commercial Operation Date shall occur no later than June 30, 2029 June 30, 2029, subject to the milestone extension mechanisms set forth herein. Seller shall be entitled to extensions of the Outside COD Date as follows: (a) one extension of up to one hundred eighty (180) days for delays in reservoir stimulation completion caused by subsurface conditions materially different from the pre-drill geologic survey, as documented by a licensed geothermal engineer; (b) additional extensions tied to active FERC or COGCC regulatory proceedings, without cap on duration; (c) Force Majeure extensions as defined in § 12.4; provided that Seller provides written notice of each trigger within fifteen (15) days of occurrence. The Parties acknowledge that EGS greenfield development carries inherent geological uncertainty not present in conventional geothermal projects, and extension provisions shall be interpreted accordingly.

// SCHEDULING IMPLEMENTATION — EGS GREENFIELD COD STRUCTURE
Project Breckenridge is Fervo's first EGS greenfield in Colorado and the first without pre-existing production well data to anchor COD confidence. The standard 90-day per-extension cap used in prior PPAs (e.g., Project Red Station 1) is insufficient here — Colorado formation uncertainty warrants a single 180-day geological delay extension plus open-ended regulatory extensions. The explicit acknowledgment of EGS geological uncertainty in the final sentence is intentional: it establishes interpretive context for any future dispute over extension triggers. Cross-reference required to § 12.4 (Force Majeure) and § 12.6 (Reservoir Performance) to ensure no overlap in remedy triggers.
